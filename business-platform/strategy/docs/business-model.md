# Business Model

This document captures the current working assumptions for the business platform.

## Core Assumption

The platform should become the operating system for the business, not just a public website. It should eventually support:

- lead capture
- qualification
- pipeline and sales visibility
- client onboarding
- service delivery tracking
- communication records
- content and marketing support
- reporting and decision support
- internal SOP execution

## Target Outcome

One platform where the business owner and team can see:

- who the customers are
- what stage each deal or client is in
- what work is active
- what needs attention today
- what revenue and delivery capacity look like

## Likely User Roles

- owner / admin
- sales or intake
- operations manager
- delivery lead
- client

## Revenue Logic

The business platform should support whichever of these models apply:

- service packages
- retainers
- project-based work
- digital products
- subscriptions or memberships

## Key Questions To Refine Later

- What is the primary offer?
- Who is the first paying user of the platform?
- Which workflow creates the most current pain?
- What must be manual at first and what should be automated early?
