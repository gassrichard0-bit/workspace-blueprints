# Ideal Customer Profile

This platform is being shaped first for a small service-based business that needs one operating system instead of many disconnected tools.

## Best-Fit Business

- founder-led or operator-led
- small team
- service delivery is a major revenue source
- sales, onboarding, and fulfillment are still partly manual
- client state is hard to see in one place

## Best-Fit Operating Problems

- leads are tracked inconsistently
- follow-ups get missed
- client onboarding is not standardized
- delivery status is unclear unless someone asks around
- reporting depends on manual checking
- SOPs exist, but adoption is inconsistent

## Why This Customer Buys

They do not mainly want "software."
They want:

- fewer dropped balls
- clearer follow-up
- cleaner team accountability
- better visibility into revenue and delivery
- one place to run the business day to day

## Anti-ICP

Not first priority:

- large enterprises
- product-led SaaS companies with mature internal systems
- businesses that mainly need accounting software
- teams needing heavy enterprise security and permission complexity on day one
