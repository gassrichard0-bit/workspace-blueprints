# 2026 Q2 MVP Foundation

## Objective

Ship the first internal-use version of the business platform that can manage the business from lead through active delivery.

## Priority Outcomes

1. centralize lead and pipeline tracking
2. standardize client onboarding
3. make active delivery visible
4. give the owner one dashboard for daily decisions

## Must-Have Modules

- dashboard
- leads
- pipeline
- clients
- onboarding
- delivery tracker

## Should-Have If Time Allows

- notes timeline
- reminders / next actions
- lightweight reporting filters

## Not In This Milestone

- billing automation
- advanced client portal
- deep workflow automation
- complex team permissions
