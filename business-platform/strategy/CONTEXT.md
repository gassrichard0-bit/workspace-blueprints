# Strategy

This workspace defines what business we are building, who it serves, how it makes money, and which platform capabilities matter most.

## What to Load

| Task | Load These | Skip These |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Define business model | `docs/business-model.md`, `vision/business-platform-vision.md` | product specs, platform docs |
| Prioritize roadmap | `vision/business-platform-vision.md`, relevant files in `roadmap/` | platform implementation details |
| Clarify customer or offer | `docs/business-model.md` | technical docs |

## Folder Structure

```text
strategy/
├── CONTEXT.md
├── docs/
├── vision/
└── roadmap/
```

## The Process

1. Clarify the business outcome.
2. Define the customer, offer, and operating model.
3. Turn strategic decisions into product priorities.
4. Push approved priorities downstream to `../product/workflows/01-briefs/`.

## Skills & Tools

| Tool | When | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Web Search | Market or competitor validation | Verify current market assumptions |
| spreadsheets / xlsx | Financial modeling | Build planning and revenue models |
| presentations / pptx | Strategic planning decks | Turn strategy into reviewable presentations |

## What NOT to Do

- Do not write implementation details here.
- Do not create technical specs in strategy.
- Do not treat ideas as roadmap commitments until they are prioritized.
