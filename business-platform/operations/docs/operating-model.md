# Operating Model

The platform should support the real operating cadence of the business.

## Main Business Loops

1. lead arrives
2. lead is qualified
3. opportunity progresses or closes
4. client is onboarded
5. service work is delivered
6. follow-up, renewal, upsell, or closeout happens

## Operating Requirement

Every major business loop should have:

- an owner
- a trigger
- a visible current status
- a next action
- a defined completion condition

## Rule For SOPs

SOPs should be specific enough that a team member or agent can run the process without guessing.
