# Lead Management SOP

## Trigger

A new lead is submitted or manually added.

## Owner

Sales, intake, or owner.

## Steps

1. create or verify lead record
2. set source and service interest
3. assign initial stage
4. add first note
5. set next follow-up date
6. advance, nurture, or close the lead

## Completion Condition

The lead is either actively progressing with a next action or clearly marked lost / closed.
