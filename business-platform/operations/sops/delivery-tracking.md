# Delivery Tracking SOP

## Trigger

Client onboarding is complete and active delivery starts.

## Owner

Operations manager or delivery lead.

## Steps

1. create engagement record
2. define first milestones or delivery items
3. assign owners and due dates
4. review progress regularly
5. flag blockers immediately
6. close or renew the engagement at completion

## Completion Condition

All active work has a visible owner, current status, and next action.
