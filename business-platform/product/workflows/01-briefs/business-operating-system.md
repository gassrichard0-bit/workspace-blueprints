# Business Operating System

## Problem

The business lacks one reliable place to manage the full lifecycle from lead to delivery to operational follow-up.

## Proposal

Build the first version of a business operating system that combines CRM, client onboarding, delivery tracking, and owner visibility into one platform.

## Scope

Included in the initial concept:

- lead intake
- opportunity pipeline
- client records
- onboarding workflow
- delivery status overview
- dashboard with core business signals

Explicitly excluded for the first version:

- full billing engine
- advanced marketing automation
- complex permissions model
- client self-service portal beyond basic status visibility

## Business Outcome

Reduce operational chaos and create a single source of truth for business activity.

## Core User

Business owner or operator running a service-based business.

## Open Questions

- Is the first release owner-only or multi-user?
- Which workflow is the highest priority: sales, onboarding, or delivery?
- What existing tools will this replace first?
