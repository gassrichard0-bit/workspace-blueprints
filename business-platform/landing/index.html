FAQ

Common questions.

How does the video process work?

You record a raw talking-head video (even on your phone) or send us a podcast link. You upload it to your Client Portal. Our AI extracts the most engaging clips, writes the hooks, and formats them for short-form platforms.

What platforms do you write for?

We optimize video for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels. We repurpose the transcripts into written content for LinkedIn and Twitter/X.

What if I don't like a hook or edit?

Request a change — we'll redo it. Growth and Video Authority plans include unlimited revisions. Starter includes 2 revision rounds per month.

Do I need to be a professional editor?

Not at all. You just need to press record. We handle the creative direction, clipping, and copy. You just review and approve.

How fast do I get my first assets?

Within 48 hours of uploading your raw footage to the Client Portal. Most clients receive their first batch the next business day.

Is there a contract?

No contracts. Pay month to month. Cancel anytime with 7 days notice. We're confident you'll stay because the content works.
